Masai Mara – Big Game Safari

  • Lodge
  • 3 days / 2 nights
  • Mid-range
Book this safari Customise itinerary

Overview

Experience the greatest wildlife spectacle on earth. This three-day Masai Mara safari takes you deep into Kenya's most famous reserve, where lions, elephants, buffalo, leopard and rhino roam the golden plains. Game drives are led by expert driver-guides in comfortable 4x4 Land Cruisers with pop-up roofs for unbeatable viewing.

Day by day

Day 1 — Nairobi to Masai Mara. Depart Nairobi after breakfast, descending into the Great Rift Valley with a stop at the viewpoint. Arrive in time for lunch and an afternoon game drive.

Day 2 — Full day in the Mara. Morning and afternoon game drives with optional hot-air balloon safari. All meals at your lodge.

Day 3 — Masai Mara to Nairobi. Early morning game drive, then return to Nairobi arriving late afternoon.
